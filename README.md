# Diamitani Music

**Industry:** Diamitani Industries

Diamitani Music provides record label, distribution, licensing, management, and creative services to independent and signed musicians.

We operate as an umbrella of companies under the Diamitani Music flagship brand:

1. **Diamitani Music** (Holding)
2. **Diamitani Management** (Management Co.)
3. **Diamitani Publishing** (Publishing Co.)
4. **Diamitani Music Distribution** (Distribution Co.)
5. **Diamitani Creative** (Creative Marketing Services)
6. **Diamitani Records** (Record Label)

These divisions can also operate as departments within a single company.

## Principal Artist

**Patrick Diamitani** is a hip-hop/EDM logo producer whose sound is styled after Flume, Calvin Harris, Immortal Technique, Lupe Fiasco, Big Sean, ASAP Ferg, and Kanye West.

### Goals

- Grow streams through logo music playlists on Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube, and Pandora.
- Secure creative licensing deals in TV and film.
- Expand distribution through TikTok, Instagram, and Snap Reels partnerships.
- Build a music catalogue worth millions of dollars.
- Establish partnerships with sub-artists to generate royalties.
- Perform as a DJ, playing pre-manufactured sets or live production mixes.
